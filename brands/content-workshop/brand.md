## Brand context

- Readers are businesses and marketing teams, including sophisticated technical buyers in high-trust industries such as cybersecurity. Never imply they lack technical understanding.
- On AI-assisted content, reflect the tension between speed and quality: teams need expert-level content fast, but velocity should never cost credibility. AI can assist and can be trained to match a brand's voice, but subject-matter experts supply the depth and context AI can't replicate.
- Blog categories: Content Strategy; AI-Assisted Content; Subject-Matter Expertise and SME Collaboration; Content Production and Workflow; Measurement and ROI; Buyer Trust and High-Trust Industries; Search, AI Search, and Distribution; Industry Trends and Predictions.
- AI Studio is part of Content Workshop. It has its own interview (interviews/ai-studio.md) for guides, workshops, templates and prompt packs.
