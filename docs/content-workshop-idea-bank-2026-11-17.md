## 2. Idea Cards

### A. Sourdough starter, not a crockpot

**Proposed title:** Your AI Content System Is a Sourdough Starter, Not a Crockpot
**Alternate titles:**
- Why AI Content Systems Need Fresh Human Input to Stay on Brand
- There's No "Set It and Forget It" in AI Content
**Angle:** An AI content system only stays brand-accurate if humans keep feeding it new input: interviews at the start, then feedback that updates its rules over time.
**Target reader:** Marketing leads and content managers running AI-assisted content across one or more brands
**Search intent:** Thought leadership. Keywords: "AI content brand voice," "maintaining AI content quality," "AI content workflow human input"
**Supporting material:**
- Headline, in their words: "building an AI system isn't a set it and forget it situation like a crockpot. It's more like a sourdough starter that needs fresh feed" (Section 9)
- "human content needs to happen in the middle to refresh the agent. nothing can run without a human input." (Section 9)
- The feedback loop: "feedback goes back to the ai agent to update the editorial addendum and writing rules" (Q5.1)
**Evidence status:** Ready
**Suggested contributor:** JB, David
**Priority:** High
**Priority rationale:** It's a memorable metaphor, the interviewee picked it, it's cleared to publish, and the workflow answer (Q5.1) supports it.
**Still needed:** One real example of an AI system drifting without fresh input, and what fixed it.
